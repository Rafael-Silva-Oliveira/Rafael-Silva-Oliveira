# Hello!

**Clinical Bioinformatician | Ph.D. Candidate in Medical Technology (NTNU)**

## About Me

I'm a Clinical Bioinformatician by training with a background spanning Biology (BSc), Molecular Biomedicine & Applied Statistics (postgraduate diplomas), and Clinical Bioinformatics (MSc) with a major in Clinical Decision Support.

I'm currently pursuing a **Ph.D. in Medical Technology at NTNU**, where I focus on stratifying small-cell lung cancer patients by cancer subtype and identifying novel biomarkers. My goal is to improve patient stratification by uncovering mechanisms of treatment resistance and vulnerabilities to improve both treatment outcomes and prognosis.

## What I Work On

- **Biomarker discovery** — hunting for biomarkers predictive of treatment response/resistance, prognosis, and diagnosis in Oncology
- **Translational research** — applying Clinical Bioinformatics, Biomedical Data Science, and Medical Statistics in Transcriptomics (T0–T3)
- **Software as Medical Device (SaMD)** — building Clinical Decision Support Systems using ML, LLMs, Computer Vision, and (radio)transcriptomics / Spatial Transcriptomics applied to Oncology
